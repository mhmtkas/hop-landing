Why no streaks?

Because streaks punish the exact brains that need the most grace. Hop’s journey counter never resets, and we say “welcome back” — never “you missed 3 days.” It’s built on the science of executive function and task chunking, not productivity-guru hustle.

FAQ

Good questions

Is Hop free?

Yes. Hop is free to use, with unlimited offline task breakdown. Hop+ unlocks unlimited AI smart-splitting and premium focus tools. No ads, ever — even on the free plan.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Rule-based task breakdown works offline and free, always. Smart (AI) splitting needs a connection.

Is my data private?

Hop has no ads and no tracking. Smart (AI) splitting sends the text you choose to split to our server to process it — you opt in first. Everything else stays on your device, and there’s no account or email required.

Which devices?

Hop is available on iPhone, with Apple Watch and home-screen widgets. Android is coming soon.

Do I need to sign up?

No. No account, no email, no password. Open the app and go.
